# Event / interaction page template

One record per event. Properties: Event, Date, Type, Companies (many), People (many), Event Link. Leave unknown details blank. Context links are not evidence that the user attended, met someone or received a referral.

## Overview

Purpose, host/location if known, attendance/source context and a short summary.

## General insights

Themes and useful observations across the event. Separate speaker claims, interpretation and unknowns; insights need no person/company assignment.

## Companies and people in context

Link relevant existing records. Identify organizer, participating company, speaker, attendee or person actually met when supported. A personal conversation stays on its People page with a reference back to this event. Avoid duplicating the event's general notes into every person.

## Follow-ups

Action, owner and date only when established; label suggestions. Keep current person-specific next actions in People properties and preserve event context here. Drafting is not sending.

## Sources and raw notes

Preserve notes, agenda, slides, recording or public links. Retain event date, speaker/context and uncertainty when a finding is reused in a company brief. A speaker's view is not automatically employer policy.

See the [fictional event](../examples/fictional-case/outputs/event-page.md). This is a copyable page structure, not a claim that a native Notion default database template is installed.

Companies and People are many-to-many relations with reciprocal Events properties. Linking a person does not replace that person's own Company associations. See the [canonical cardinality table](../docs/database-schema.md#relations).
