## EX-E01 — equipment checkout handoff pilot

- Context: Cedar Tool Library, a fictional community equipment-lending service; one desk.
- Role: Desk assistant, September 2028–February 2029. The desk supervisor owned operating policy and approved changes.
- Problem: Equipment handoffs were slow when borrowers could not locate the correct labels and checklist.
- Personal actions: Recorded 28 checkout observations; grouped recurring points of confusion; proposed clearer shelf labels and a shorter handoff checklist with two desk volunteers.
- Outcome: During a three-week, one-desk pilot, median handoff time was 11 minutes versus an 18-minute baseline. The supervisor's timing sheet used the same start/end definition.
- Limits: Assisted the pilot; did not lead the organization or change every location. No retention, revenue, causal experiment or company-wide outcome was measured. The data describes observed medians, not a controlled causal estimate.
- Fictional evidence reference: FIX-E01, a synthetic observation/timing summary represented by this entry. No underlying real dataset exists.
