# Six-section company intelligence framework

Use these headings for a full profile. Quick compresses all six; Standard explains the central mechanisms; Deep investigates competing explanations and tradeoffs. State Unknown or Not applicable rather than inventing coverage. The executive brief precedes the profile; see [skill instructions](../SKILL.md).

## 1. Company snapshot

Explain what is sold, to whom and for what problem. Distinguish user, buyer and payer; identify relevant parent, business unit and geography. Describe the revenue model and dated scale/stage evidence where available. Select a few memorable facts without repeating them in every section. A mission statement alone does not describe a business.

## 2. Strategy and business model

Explain revenue streams, the main growth mechanism, costs/margin constraints and investment priorities. Identify the economic engine and strategic tension: for example, standardized subscription delivery versus costly customer-specific onboarding. Tie management's stated ambition to evidence of resource allocation and traction, keeping projections attributed. State risks, alternatives and indicators that would strengthen or weaken the thesis. Missing private-company economics remain unknown.

## 3. Product and customer understanding

Trace a key customer workflow from entry to value: main steps, buyer/user differences, friction, choice and switching considerations. At Standard/Deep depth explain one important product's value, monetization and tradeoffs. Distinguish shipped, pilot, announced and speculative features; do not imply firsthand use when none occurred. A proposed improvement must be labeled a hypothesis with a user problem, business objective, validation approach and success measure. Optional industry lenses come from the user's private setup, not this general framework.

## 4. Market and competitive landscape

Define the relevant segment before market sizing. Compare a few direct competitors and substitutes, including manual/in-house alternatives where relevant, on the same customer/workflow dimensions. Attribute market estimates with date/scope/methodology, or explain structure qualitatively when numbers are weak. Identify where the company may win, where alternatives are stronger and what evidence supports the distinction. A feature is not automatically a defensible moat.

## 5. Recent developments and business implications

Cover the latest available earnings or material business update with its reporting period and announcement date. Private firms may have no disclosed financials. Select significant product/pricing changes, partnerships, transactions, organization changes, material technology bets and challenges. Use date / change / why it matters / what to watch. Distinguish launch from announcement, transaction agreement from closing, allegation from finding, and company-wide change from a specific team's fate. Surface a few informed discussion topics rather than scripted interview answers.

## 6. Organization, culture and PM hiring

Cover business/product organization, publicly supported leaders/reporting lines and PM decision rights; leave nonpublic structure unknown. Explain collaboration with engineering, design, data, operations and commercial teams when evidenced. Distinguish product management from program/project/product-marketing responsibilities.

Explicitly cover stated values, evidence of working culture and general qualities sought in candidates. Translate supported expectations into work behavior without presenting corporate values as an internal interview scorecard. Separate employer branding, scoped employee anecdotes and observed evidence; one profile or review cannot establish company-wide culture.

Use current official program pages, PM JDs and recruiting guidance for hiring. Distinguish internship/early-career from experienced hiring and attach team, geography, level and cycle where known. Separate official requirements from preferred qualifications and inferred patterns. Historical candidate accounts stay dated/anecdotal. Exact-role eligibility is checked only when relevant; unknown is not ineligible. Detailed candidate fit and people discovery belong to separate requests.

## Refresh record

For a saved finding keep source URL/title, publication/event date, observation date, supported fact, analysis/business implication, uncertainty and affected section. Preserve older notes and explicitly mark superseded claims. Section review dates cover only sources actually revalidated. Use the existing Company record, whose Jobs/People links remain intact.
