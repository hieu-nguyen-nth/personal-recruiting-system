# Company Intelligence — reference implementation

Start with a company name or URL. A JD, resume and open job are optional; company understanding is the primary task. Resolve entity, parent/subsidiary, geography and business unit before combining evidence. Reuse existing notes as leads to revalidate, not proof that a claim remains current.

## Depth and routing

| Mode | Coverage and output |
|---|---|
| Quick / Basic | Executive brief and compact coverage of all six sections; unknowns explicit |
| Standard — default when neither requested nor saved | Business mechanics, a key product workflow, competition, recent moves and PM operating/hiring model |
| Deep | Standard plus triangulated strategic bets, economics, product tradeoffs and relevant team evidence |
| Refresh / One insight | Update affected claims at the existing depth; do not produce an unsolicited full report |

Respect requested depth first, then saved depth. Basic maps to Quick; do not overwrite existing preferences. Research depth describes effort and evidence, not a paid tool or word-count guarantee. Read the [six-section framework](references/company-framework.md) for full profiles and substantive refreshes; consult only the relevant section for a single insight.

## Research method

1. Establish the business, products, customers and latest available earnings or material operating update. Then investigate strategic change, alternatives and PM organization. Keep company-wide evidence separate from team/role evidence.
2. Open current primary sources: filings/earnings for financials, official product/pricing/releases for availability and workflow, current careers/JDs/guides for hiring. Challenge consequential claims with credible independent evidence where accessible. Search snippets are leads, not reviewed sources.
3. Prioritize recent material moves. Record event, publication and observation dates; distinguish announced, launched and completed events. For each move explain what changed → business implication → what to watch.
4. Separate Fact, Analysis and Unknown. Preserve metric period, units, geography/segment and definition; distinguish revenue from transaction volume, targets from results and management intent from traction. Attribute estimates and forecasts. Explain alternatives to consequential inferences.
5. Lead with a concise executive brief, then six numbered sections. State the scope/date/depth and material source gaps. No browsing means a dated source-bounded draft, not live research. Do not fill sections with unsupported detail to meet a length target.

## Output and knowledge-base maintenance

The executive brief explains the company in plain language, strategic tension, important changes, PM lens and what to watch/start reading. Follow with the framework's six sections and claim-level source references. Keep facts near implications and avoid repeating a news list across sections.

Label hiring evidence Official—specific role/program, Official—company-wide, Inferred, Anecdotal or Unknown. Company values, actual culture evidence, general hiring qualities and one role's requirements are different claims. A senior PM posting does not establish an internship program; silence does not establish eligibility.

Research-only requests can return in chat. When saving is requested/configured, fetch the Companies schema and existing company page first. Keep the brief, six sections and dated insights in that existing record; no parallel library. Preserve user notes/history and explicitly supersede changed claims. Update only reviewed sections' dates. Verify the write before calling it saved. See the [page template](../../templates/company-page.md).

Company knowledge remains reusable across Jobs. Candidate-fit scores, resume evidence mapping, people discovery, interview coaching, outreach and scheduling are separate workflows. A single article should result in one affected-section insight, not trigger the entire recruiting pipeline.
