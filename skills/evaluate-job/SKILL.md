# Evaluate a job — generic sample

Read the exact JD, source/date, current job page, relevant company brief, private goals and candidate evidence. Retrieve the full posting before a full assessment. Canonical employer/requisition identity takes precedence over an aggregator mirror. A quick screen can identify likely scope match/exclusion; label it Quick screen rather than Full evaluation.

Assess the work, not the title alone. Use the user's own framework if provided. Otherwise use these qualitative dimensions without inventing personal weights:

| Dimension | What to compare |
|---|---|
| Industry / customer | Domain exposure and customer understanding; distinguish transferable experience from deep expertise |
| Function | Actual duties, decision ownership and evidence of performing the relevant work |
| Problem / product | Similar user problem, product surface, complexity and operating context |
| Career value | Incremental learning, mentorship, deliverable and alignment with the user's next step |
| Feasibility | Required education/experience, dates, location and explicit authorization conditions |

Use Strong / Partial / Gap / Unknown with a short reason and source. Separate recommendation from user priority. Unknown sponsorship, team assignment or return-offer potential is not proof of acceptance/rejection. A title or famous company alone establishes neither fit nor career value. If a user-supplied scoring method has unknown inputs, preserve them rather than normalizing them away silently.

Return a concise decision, supporting assessment, hard blockers vs unknowns, and the next fact most likely to change it. The [fictional example](../../docs/worked-example.md) shows industry and functional fit leading to different conclusions. Do not rewrite the resume during evaluation.

Before saving, inspect earlier assessments and application status. New discoveries get screened; fully evaluate viable/unclear roles. For a backlog, bound the batch, report coverage/deferred items and leave inaccessible JDs unfinished. Save the assessment, sources, review date/state and next action without changing application status or personal priority. An eligibility uncertainty can coexist with a completed assessment; Needs info applies when information prevents completing the assessment itself.
