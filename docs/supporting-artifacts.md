# Supporting artifacts

Keep these in a private recruiting workspace, outside this repository. They are inputs to the skills, not extra Notion databases. Start with the evidence you already have; a story bank and bullet bank can be sections of one file until separate files are useful. Link their locations and current versions from your private setup note.

| Artifact | What belongs in it | Used by |
|---|---|---|
| Master resume | Full verified career chronology, titles, dates, education and accomplishments; broader than a one-page application | Evaluation for background; tailoring for selection; QA for consistency |
| Story bank | Detailed situation/problem, personal responsibility, actions/tradeoffs, outcome, metric scope, dates, evidence and caveats for each achievement | Tailoring and QA; company/interview preparation |
| Bullet bank | Concise alternative phrasings linked to story IDs; label proposed vs approved wording | Tailoring. A bullet variant is not independent proof of its own claim |
| Skills inventory | Tools/methods, actual proficiency and evidence of use; distinguish learning, prototype and production work | Tailoring and QA, especially additional-skills sections |
| Recruiting playbook | Private goals, target roles, industry/function preferences, decision rules and constraints | Evaluation and prioritization. Shared samples contain only general dimensions |
| Company brief + JD | Sourced company context and the exact opening's responsibilities/criteria, kept on their Notion pages | Evaluation, company research, tailoring and QA |
| Resume template, if used | Approved structure/layout; preserve the original and create role-specific copies | Optional document preparation and final-file QA |
| Application outputs | Exact proposed edits, current draft, QA report and approved export if produced | Job-page continuation and human review; these do not replace source banks |

## Keep facts and wording distinct

A useful story entry has a stable local ID and states scope precisely. This entirely fictional example is safe to copy as a format:

```text
Story ID: EX-S01
Context: Customer onboarding for a fictional workflow-software product.
Responsibility: Owned the onboarding experiment; did not own the entire platform.
Actions: Interviewed 9 administrators and simplified the setup checklist with design.
Outcome: In a 42-account pilot, median setup time fell from 7 to 4.5 days.
Limits: Pilot only; no revenue or company-wide retention result measured.
Evidence: Private experiment notes and measurement definition.
```

A bullet variant can reference `EX-S01` and emphasize research or experimentation, while retaining the same numbers, role and pilot qualifier. The [worked example](worked-example.md) follows this evidence into a proposed bullet and QA finding. All of its facts are synthetic and must not be used as real resume claims.

## Source precedence and updates

Use explicit current user corrections and the latest authoritative evidence before older resume phrasing or a derived bullet. If sources conflict and there is no confirmed correction, flag the conflict rather than choosing the stronger number. A strategy preference can change which achievement to select; it cannot change the achievement's facts.

Choose one canonical location per artifact and record the version/date. A connected snapshot is a dated reference, not another editable master. Refresh snapshots when sources change; do not assume a new chat or cloud runtime can read a local path. Keep a short source reference beside proposed changes so QA can trace them without duplicating entire private banks into every output.
